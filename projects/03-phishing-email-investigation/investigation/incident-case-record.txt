--------------------------------------------------
ANALYST CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------

The email was classified as suspicious based on urgency and
credential-verification language, an embedded login URL, and a mismatch
between the sender and Reply-To domains.

The custom email analyzer generated Windows Event ID 3101, which was
ingested by Wazuh and triggered custom Rule 100301 at Level 10, mapped
to MITRE ATT&CK T1566.002.

Endpoint investigation identified Microsoft Edge performing a DNS query
for the embedded domain. DNS resolution failed, and no corresponding
browser-history entry or Sysmon network connection was found. No
credential submission or malicious execution was observed.

Successful user interaction and endpoint compromise were therefore
not established.

--------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDED RESPONSE
--------------------------------------------------

- Remove/quarantine the suspicious email.
- Block confirmed malicious sender/domain/URL indicators when applicable.
- Search for additional recipients or related messages.
- Review endpoint and account telemetry for further suspicious activity.
- Verify whether the recipient interacted with the message.
- Reset credentials and revoke sessions if credential submission is confirmed.
- Isolate the endpoint if evidence of endpoint compromise is discovered.
- Preserve email, SIEM, endpoint and timeline evidence.
